Visiting

Is Durga Puja free to visit?

Yes. Experiencing Durga Puja is free — walk in, see the idol, and join the celebration. Parking is free. Donations are welcome.

For lunch and other activities, take a ticket. That is optional; it is how we fund the puja as a non-profit.

When and where is Durga Puja 2026?

17–21 October 2026 at Valid World Hall, Carrer de Buenaventura Muñoz 6, Barcelona.

A free cultural programme follows on Friday 23 October at 18:00 at Federació d’Entitats, Carrer de Calàbria 66.

Exact puja timings in Barcelona are announced closer to the event. View the schedule · Durga Puja 2026

Can anyone come?

Yes. You do not need to be Bengali or Hindu. Neighbours, friends, and anyone curious are welcome.

Children are welcome. Under 5s are free. Ages 5–12 have a child ticket if they want lunch and activities.

How do I get there? Is parking free?

Main puja: Carrer de Buenaventura Muñoz 6 — Metro Arc de Triomf (L1). Parking at the venue is free.

Cultural programme: Carrer de Calàbria 66 — Metro Rocafort (L1).

Tickets and donations

What do tickets include, and how much do they cost?

Tickets are for guaranteed entry, lunch, and other activities. They range from €35 to €65.

Ticket Price

Children up to 5 years Free

All tickets except the day pass are valid for every day of the puja (17–21 October 2026).

How do I register and pay?

Fill in the registration form, then transfer the total to Bengali Cultural Association (Santander). In the transfer concept write your name and the word Donation.

You will get a confirmation email. Tickets follow after we verify the payment.

Are donations welcome?

Yes. We are a registered non-profit, and the celebration stays free to visit because people contribute when they can. You can add an optional donation on the registration form, or walk in without buying a ticket.

Durga Puja

What is Durga Puja?

It is Bengal’s autumn homecoming: Durga as a daughter returning to her parents for five loud, well-fed days — and as the ten-armed goddess who defeats the demon Mahishasura.

The longer story lives on the Durga Puja 2026 page.

Is it only a religious festival?

No. In Kolkata it is built in public: pandals, lighting, food, and neighbourhood art that anyone can walk through, regardless of faith. That is how we hold it in Barcelona too.

UNESCO Inscribed “Durga Puja in Kolkata” in December 2021. ich.unesco.org

Ajuntament de Barcelona The Office of Religious Affairs explains Durga Puja on the city site (article from October 2024). That piece is about the festival in Barcelona; it does not list BCA’s 2026 dates or venue. ajuntament.barcelona.cat

What will I see at the event?

The Durga idol (hand-built in Kumartuli, Kolkata), pandal decorations, puja ceremonies, an Indian food stall, cultural performances, dhunuchi dance, and sindur khela on Vijaya Dashami.

  • 10 Oct — Mahalaya (invocation; before the five days)
  • 17 Oct — Maha Shashthi (unveiling)
  • 18 Oct — Maha Saptami
  • 19 Oct — Maha Ashtami
  • 20 Oct — Maha Navami
  • 21 Oct — Vijaya Dashami (farewell)

About BCA

What is BCA, and how do I get in touch?

The Bengali Cultural Association Barcelona is a registered non-profit founded in 2022. Durga Puja is our loudest week; we also mark Poila Boisakh, Rabindra Jayanti, and gatherings through the year.

Email info@bcaspain.org, or use the contact page. If you want to help, come to an event and say hello — there is no separate membership form beyond puja registration.
