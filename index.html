What is BCA?

A cultural home for Bengalis — and anyone who feels welcome in the circle.

The Bengali Cultural Association of India in Spain is a non-profit founded in 2022. We exist to preserve and share Bengali culture in Barcelona: the festivals, yes — but also the quieter things that make a place feel like home.

Think of us as a living-room for the diaspora: a place to sing, cook, argue about football, teach children a few lines of Bangla, and meet neighbours who understand why mustard oil matters.

Bengal ⟷ Catalunya

We didn't choose Barcelona by accident.

In many ways, it already speaks our language. Bengali and Catalan are two cultures fiercely devoted to their poets, their language, and their festivals — an ocean apart, and closer than they look.

Bengal

Language, fought for

On 21 February 1952, students in Dhaka died defending the right to speak Bengali. The date is now UNESCO's International Mother Language Day, marked by Bengalis everywhere.

Catalunya

Llengua, sostinguda

Català survived decades of suppression to remain the living, spoken language of these streets — a language its people simply refused to let go quiet.

Bengal

Water lets go

Durga Puja ends with visarjan: the goddess is carried to the water and released, so devotion can return again next year.

Catalunya

Fire lets go

The Revetlla de Sant Joan ends with bonfires on the shortest night — Barcelona lets the old year burn, so the new one can begin.

Bengal

Songs that carried a nation

Rabindranath Tagore wrote the words that became the national anthems of both India and Bangladesh — a poet doing a nation's remembering for it.

Catalunya

Songs that carried a movement

The Nova Cançó singers of the 1960s, like Lluís Llach, turned Catalan song into the sound of a culture refusing to disappear.

Bengal

Built by hand, together

Every idol from Kumartuli is shaped by hands trained since childhood — one family teaching the next, a season at a time.

Catalunya

Raised by hand, together

Every human tower at a festa major is built the same way — generations standing on each other's shoulders, literally, until it holds.

BCA is where these two devotions meet — not as metaphor, but as two communities in Barcelona learning each other's names for the same thing: home, kept alive by ritual.
